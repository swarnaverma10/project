My Love ❤️

2 years... jab peeche dekhti hoon toh realise hota hai ki humne kitni saari cheezein saath experience ki hain. Beautiful memories, ups and downs, achhe bure din aur difficult situations. Aur phir bhi hum yahan tak saath aaye hain.

Mujhe pata hai life hamesha easy nahi hogi. Kabhi situations difficult hongi, kabhi cheezein hamare according nahi hongi. But jo bhi hoga, hum saath deal karenge, dheere-dheere, step by step.

Hum dono kabhi-kabhi chhoti baaton par disturb ho jaate hain, misunderstandings ho jaati hain. But main chahti hoon hum ek doosre ko better samjhein, communicate karein, patience rakhein aur apne relationship ka spark kabhi lose na karein.

Ab hum sirf ek doosre ke liye nahi, humare liye, humare bond aur future ke liye kaam karenge. Apne dreams achieve karenge, khud ko better banayenge aur saath mein grow karenge.

Honestly, mera time tere saath bahut beautiful raha hai aur mujhe pata hai aage aur bhi achha hoga. Main bahut mehnat kar rahi hoon taaki jaldi tujhse mil paaun. I know tu bhi mere liye bahut efforts karta hai, and I appreciate everything. Kabhi-kabhi cheezein ajeeb ho jaati hain, but hum milkar sab better karenge.

Distance hai, but tu mujhse door nahi hai. Main khud ko bahut samjhati hoon ki ye bas ek phase hai. I miss you so much, bahut zyada. Bas mann karta hai tujhe hug karun, tere paas rahun aur tere saath time spend karun.

I love you so much. Main tere saath bahut zyada attached hoon, jitna explain bhi nahi kar sakti. Please never leave me. I don't want to lose you. I want you in my life, always.

Abhi toh sirf 2 years hue hain. Abhi bahut saari memories banani hain, jagah ghoomni hain, celebrations, silly fights, laughter aur poori life saath experience karni hai.

I want you to be my partner. I want to grow with you, build our future together and keep choosing you, even on difficult days.

Let's take things slowly, understand each other and always work for US. I want this bond to stay beautiful forever.

I love you, I miss you, and I want us. Always and forever. ❤️

Happy 2nd Anniversary, my favourite person.

Always yours ❤️
